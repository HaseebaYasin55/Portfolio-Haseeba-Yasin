01 · about

From chatbot personas to production agents

A quick, honest look at how I actually got here — traced through the real projects I shipped, in the order I shipped them.

I got into applied AI the way most engineers do — by building something small and slightly ridiculous. Personality Bot was a Streamlit chatbot that could switch between a Doctor, a Chef, and a Maths Teacher persona while letting me A/B test different LLMs live. It taught me the fundamentals of prompt layering and model behavior — and it's where this portfolio's timeline starts.

From there, the projects got progressively more "production-shaped": grounding answers in real documents with Adaptive RAG (and actually measuring retrieval latency and hallucination, not just demoing it), giving agents persistent memory with Mem0, and giving them a voice — first with Groq Whisper + Deepgram, then with a full Vapi-powered voice-RAG stack backed by Supabase.

Along the way I built multi-tool agents that search, transcribe and summarize video on their own, a CrewAI pipeline of three cooperating agents that researches news and reports to Slack, and — most recently — an MCP server that lets Claude Desktop manage a real to-do list through typed tools instead of a chat hack.

I care about the parts most demos skip: grounding, tool-calling reliability, evaluation, and architecture that doesn't fall over once it leaves the notebook.
